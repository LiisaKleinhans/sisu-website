A closer look · For Teams

Team Performance

Find out what is really holding your team back, and build a plan to grow stronger together.

Connect

Team Days

Fun, active days that build rapport, celebrate wins and bring people closer, such as Amazing Race-style challenges, immersive team competitions, immersive art experiences and many more. A short debrief links the experience back to everyday teamwork.

Best for
Celebrating milestones, kicking off a new year, welcoming new team members
Time
Half or full day
Understand

Team Workshops

Interactive sessions where the team discovers each other's communication and working styles, learns to play to each other's strengths, and builds the trust that makes working together easier.

Best for
Teams that want to communicate and collaborate even better
Time
One session or a short series
Transform

Team Journey

A full process that finds the real causes of friction or stuck performance, then builds a tailored series of team sessions and practical actions to deal with them. The journey is owned by the team and its manager or leader, and we support you all the way.

Best for
Ongoing friction, low trust, or performance that won't shift
Time
Tailored to your team's needs

Built around your team. Every option is customised to what your team needs, from the activities and tools we use to the length and number of sessions. Options can also be combined, for example a workshop followed by a team day to celebrate progress.
